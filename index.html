From form to seat in five steps

Everything happens online except document verification, which you complete on campus.

  1. RegisterCreate your application and choose up to two programmes.
  2. Upload documentsAdd marksheets, certificates and a photo.
  3. Merit listRanked by marks or entrance score on 15 May.
  4. VerifyBring the originals to the admission office.
  5. ConfirmAccept your offer online to secure the seat.

Questions applicants ask

Can't find your answer? Call the helpline below.

Can I apply to more than one programme?

You can list two choices in one application. The merit list considers your first choice before your second.

What if my final results are not out yet?

Apply with your latest marksheet and upload the final one before document verification.

Can I withdraw after confirming my seat?

Yes. Contact the admission office before 30 June so the seat can go to the next applicant.

How do I correct a mistake in my application?

Send your Application ID and the correction to the admission office before the last date.
